PROJECT / SALES CONVERSION

Logitech

FACEBOOK · TIKTOK · SHOPEE · LAZADA · TIKI
MAR 2023 → FEB 2024

22.1xROASSustained over 10 months
01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Logitech is a Swiss-founded maker of computer peripherals: mice, keyboards, webcams and gaming gear.

I ran Logitech’s digital marketing across all of its e-commerce platforms. There were two goals: raise the share of revenue from online channels, and build brand awareness on social media.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Role & Scope

Campaign Planning
Set sales and marketing targets directly with the Logitech manager, then chose the channels and split the budget.
Campaign Execution
Ran keyword search ads on Shopee and Lazada, with extra traffic from Facebook. Also ran TikTok Shop livestreams, from writing the script to boosting product ads.
Budget & KPI Monitoring
Tracked spend against KPIs and moved budget between channels to hit both the sales target and the marketing goals.
Analysis & Reporting
Broke results down by channel and funnel stage, and reported what to change next.
Tracking & Measurement
Worked with Logitech’s internal team to check tracking and reconcile the numbers across Shopee Brand Portal, Lazada Seller Center, Meta and TikTok.
Creative & Conversion Optimization
Wrote creative briefs, tested audiences, and suggested changes to store pages and voucher offers to lift conversion.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Channel & Budget Strategy
Nearly 70% of the total budget went to e-commerce channels, to grow revenue while keeping ROAS above the client’s minimum of 19.5. Another 20% bought traffic and engagement to fill the middle of the funnel. The rest went to awareness, so more people knew the brand and its voucher deals.
Audience & Traffic Strategy
Ran ads for broad reach, retargeting and lookalike audiences. Retargeting was split by behavior (product view, add-to-cart, checkout, purchase) to cut wasted spend.
Campaign Optimization
On marketplace search, brand keywords came first so Logitech showed up whenever shoppers looked for it, and I pushed impression share on keywords for Logitech products. For livestreams, I tested different objective combinations to raise live views and product views, which lifted the purchase rate.
Creative & Conversion Strategy
Agreed voucher offers with the Logitech manager based on what the sales data showed.
Example: 30% voucher discount (up to VND 150K) for orders above VND 1M.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

No. 1 on Shopee
Ranked as the best-selling brand in Shopee’s Computer Accessories category during the 11.11 mega sale.
No. 1 on Lazada
Ranked as the best-selling brand in Lazada’s Computer Accessories category during the 6.6, 11.11 and 12.12 mega sales.
22.1x ROAS, above the 19.5 client target
22.1x ROAS across e-commerce marketplaces, TikTok Shop and Meta against a client target of 19.5, on an average monthly spend of VND 400 million.
Online share of revenue doubled
Online channels’ share of total revenue doubled from 10% to 20%, as sales shifted from offline to online.
