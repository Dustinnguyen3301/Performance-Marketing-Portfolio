PROJECT / SALES CONVERSION

Logitech

FACEBOOK · TIKTOK · SHOPEE · LAZADA · TIKI
2024 → JAN 2025

22.1xROASSustained over 10 months
01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Logitech is a Swiss-founded global technology brand known for high-quality computer peripherals and accessories, including mice, keyboards, webcams and gaming gear, trusted by everyday users and professionals worldwide.

I took full ownership of digital marketing operations across all e-commerce platforms, with the goal of increasing the share of revenue from online channels and building Logitech’s brand awareness on social media.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Role & Scope

Campaign Planning
Worked directly with the Logitech manager to define marketing and sales objectives and develop the media strategy, including channel selection and budget allocation.
Campaign Execution
Launched, monitored, and optimized keyword search campaigns on Shopee and Lazada, supported by external traffic from Facebook. Also ran TikTok Shop Livestreams, from writing the livestream script to boosting product ads.
Budget & KPI Monitoring
Tracked spend and KPI delivery, recommending budget adjustments based on performance and business priorities to achieve both business and marketing goals.
Analysis & Reporting
Analyzed performance across channels and conversion stages, delivering reports with actionable insights and optimization recommendations.
Tracking & Measurement
Coordinated with Logitech’s internal team to validate tracking, reconcile data, and resolve measurement discrepancies through Shopee Brand Portal, Lazada Seller Center, Meta and TikTok.
Creative & Conversion Optimization
Developed creative briefs, tested audiences, and recommended improvements to store content and voucher offers to support conversion.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Channel & Budget Strategy
Nearly 70% of the total budget went to e-commerce channels to grow revenue while keeping ROAS at the client’s expectation (minimum ROAS: 19.5). Another 20% drove traffic and user engagement, widening the middle funnel to support the lower funnel. The remainder went to awareness, so users learn about the brand and its voucher deals.
Audience & Traffic Strategy
Ran and customized ads for many audience types, from mass reach to retargeting and lookalike. For retargeting, I segmented users in depth by behavior, such as product view, add-to-cart, checkout and purchase, to improve efficiency.
Campaign Optimization
For e-commerce campaigns, I prioritized reaching customers through brand keywords to maximize visibility at every touchpoint of the customer journey, while also optimizing impression share on product keywords related to Logitech products. In livestream campaigns, I tested many goal combinations to increase livestream views and product views, which helped improve the purchase rate.
Creative & Conversion Strategy
Shared key insights and aligned with the Logitech manager to develop a range of voucher offers that add customer value and simplify the purchase experience.
Example: 30% voucher discount (up to VND 150K) for orders above VND 1M.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

No. 1 on Shopee
Ranked as the best-selling brand in Shopee’s Computer Accessories category during the 11.11 mega sale campaigns.
No. 1 on Lazada
Ranked as the best-selling brand in Lazada’s Computer Accessories category during the 6.6, 11.11, 12.12 mega sale campaigns.
22.1x ROAS, above the 19.5 client target
Achieved a ROAS of 22.1x across all channels, against the client’s target of 19.5, with an average monthly marketing investment of VND 400 million.
x2 online revenue contribution
In 2024, online channels’ share of total revenue doubled versus 2023, from 10% to 20%.
