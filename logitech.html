PROJECT / SALES CONVERSION

Logitech

FACEBOOK · TIKTOK · SHOPEE · LAZADA · TIKI
2024 -> JAN 2025

22.1xROASSustained over 10 months
01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Logitech is a Swiss-founded global technology brand known for high-quality computer peripherals and accessories, including mice, keyboards, webcams and gaming gear, trusted by everyday users and professionals worldwide.

I took full ownership of digital marketing operations across all e-commerce platforms, with the goal of increasing the share of revenue from online channels and building Logitech’s brand awareness on social media.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Roles & Scopes

Campaign Planning
Work directly with a manager of Logitech to define marketing and sales objectives and develop the media strategy, including channel selection and budget allocation.
Campaign Execution
Launched, monitored, and optimized keyword search campaigns on Shopee and Lazada with supporting of external traffic through Facebook, operate the TikTok Shop Livestream through build the livestream script, boost product ads.
Budget & KPI Monitoring
Tracked spend and KPI delivery, recommending budget adjustments based on performance and business priorities to achieve both business & marketing goal.
Analysis & Reporting
Analyzed performance across channels and conversion stages, delivering reports with actionable insights and optimization recommendations.
Tracking & Measurement
Coordinated with Logitech's internal team to validate tracking, reconcile data, and resolve measurement discrepancies through Shopee Brand Portal, Lazada Seller Center, Meta and TikTok.
Creative & Conversion Optimization
Developed creative briefs, tested audiences, and recommended improvements to store content and voucher offers to support conversion.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Channel & Budget Strategy
Nearest 70% of total budget for the E-commerce channels to increasing the revenue and maintain the ROAS get client's expectation (ROAS minimum: 19.5), alongside 20% of budget for driving more traffic and increasing user's engagements, which help expand the middle funnel to support lower funnel. And remain budget for awareness to help user know about brand and voucher deal.
Audience & Traffic Strategy
Apply & customize ads for many audience's types, generally from mass to retargeting to look-a-like audience. Including the retargeting audience, i had deeply segment by user's behavior such as product view, add-to-cart, check-out, purchase to optimize the efficiency.
Campaign Optimization
For E-commerce campaigns, i priorities approach to customer through brand keyword to maximize visibility on touchpoint of customer journey, beside optimize impression share of product keyword related to Logitech products. In the livestream campaigns, i had test a lot of goals combine to gain more livestream watch and view product, which help improve the purchase rate.
Creative & Conversion Strategy
Provide key insights & get aligned with the Logitech manager to developed a range of voucher offers to enhance customer value and simplify the purchase experience
Examples: Voucher discount 30% (maximize: VND 150K) for ordered had value > 1M.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

Ranked as the best-selling brand in Lazada's Electrical Accessories Category during the 6.6, 11.11, 12.12 mega sale campaigns.
No. 2 on Shopee
Ranked as the best-selling brand in Lazada's Electrical Accessories Category during the 11.11 mega sale campaigns.
ROAS above 16.2
Maintained the ROAS on all channels with average monnthly marketing investment of VND 400 millions.
x2 online revenue contribution
