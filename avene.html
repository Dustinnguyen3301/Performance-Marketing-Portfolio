PROJECT / SALES CONVERSION

Avène

FACEBOOK · TIKTOK · SHOPEE · LAZADA · 2024

+160%Total Ads Revenue2024 vs. 2023
01 / CONTEXT

Background & objective

Avène is a French dermocosmetics brand known for high-quality skincare recommended by healthcare professionals and distributed in Vietnam by DKSH.

Working with DKSH, I managed paid media to drive sales for Avène’s stores on Shopee and Lazada, supported by Facebook and TikTok campaigns to bring traffic to the stores.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My role & scope

Campaign Planning
Partnered with Avène’s point of contact to define marketing and sales objectives and develop the media strategy, including channel selection and budget allocation.
Campaign Execution
Launched, monitored, and optimized keyword search campaigns on Shopee and Lazada, alongside Facebook and TikTok campaigns to drive store traffic.
Budget & KPI Monitoring
Tracked spend and KPI delivery, recommending budget adjustments based on performance and business priorities.
Analysis & Reporting
Analyzed performance across channels and conversion stages, delivering reports with actionable insights and optimization recommendations.
Tracking & Measurement
Coordinated with Avène’s internal team to validate tracking, reconcile data, and resolve measurement discrepancies.
Creative & Conversion Optimization
Developed creative briefs, tested audiences, and recommended improvements to store content and voucher offers to support conversion.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & execution

Shopee-led budget allocation
Allocated over 50% of the total budget to Shopee to scale spend while maintaining ROAS, with Lazada serving as a supporting channel during mega sale campaigns.
Social traffic acquisition
Used Facebook and TikTok to drive qualified store traffic and increase store visibility.
Brand-led keyword strategy
Prioritized branded search terms across multiple match types, allocating over 60% of the e-commerce campaign budget to brand keywords, followed by product and generic keywords.
Voucher optimization
Developed a range of voucher offers to enhance customer value and simplify the purchase experience.
04 / IMPACT

Results & learnings

No. 1 on Lazada
Ranked as the best-selling brand in Lazada’s Cosmetics category during the 3.3, 6.6, 9.9 and 12.12 mega sale campaigns.
No. 2 on Shopee
Ranked as the second-best-selling brand in Shopee’s Sunscreen category during the 6.6 and 9.9 mega sale campaigns.
ROAS above 6.3x
Maintained ROAS above 6.3x with average monthly marketing spend of VND 250 million.
25% lower cost per visit
Reduced cost per store visit by 25% from March to September 2024.
160% revenue growth
Recorded total revenue of over VND 15 billion across advertising channels in 2024, with 160% growth versus 2023.
