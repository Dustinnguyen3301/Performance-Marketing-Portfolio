01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Cash Loan is Home Credit Vietnam’s flagship product: an unsecured consumer loan from 3 to 250 million VND, with approval in just 3 minutes and disbursement within 10 minutes. The goal of this project was to acquire customers online across many channels and touchpoints. I directly managed a total budget of nearly 5 billion VND per month.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Role & Scope

Campaign Planning
Partnered with stakeholders to define acquisition goals and build the media plan, from channel mix to budget split.
Campaign Execution
Owned the acquisition KPIs and directed the agency team in launching campaigns and refining them through continuous optimization.
Performance Monitoring & Analysis
Reviewed results across all channels every day, followed platform updates, and limited the effect of sudden changes and fierce market competition.
Reporting & Creative Insights
Reported full performance to stakeholders and worked with the agency team to turn data into creative insights that drive more contracts.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Master Channel Strategy
I separated the user flow into two streams: web and app. Google was the main channel, run through many campaign types, with 35% of the total budget. TikTok came next at 25%, reaching potential customers directly through short video, then Facebook at 10%. The remainder went to SEO, Zalo ZNS and affiliate vendors.
Audience Segmentation Strategy
I tested and ran ads on many audience types, mainly retargeting and lookalike, and took a direct part in segmenting Home’s customer base by demographics, credit history and loan value, often combining several conditions at once. Working with the tech team, we used several methods to build and refresh audiences, replacing manual work: manual upload, in-app events and API from Big Data.
Search Behavior & Customer Journey Optimization
I led the agency team to reach customers through many touchpoints, from native ads to active search. For search, I optimized across keyword groups: brand, product, category and competitor.
Creative Variety Testing
I collected insights and worked with stakeholders to build varied creative angles: product USPs, promotions and schemes, and benefit updates. Test results defined the winning content, which we then scaled efficiently.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

+41% Cash Loan contracts: 65K through September 2026 vs. 46K in all of 2025
Total Cash Loan contracts reached 65K in the first nine months of 2026, already above the 46K of the whole of 2025. Marketing also set a record month of 8,160 contracts, against a previous average of about 3,900.
Over 10% of total marketing budget saved
Optimizing the app flow with a mix of varied creative and audiences helped reach KPIs earlier, saving over 10% of the total marketing budget.
New app flow now delivers over 80% of contracts
Before June 2025, the program ran almost only the web flow, relying on Google SEM and Performance Max. I found a way to run the app flow as well, and it now accounts for over 80% of contracts.

Key learning: expanding beyond the original web-only flow by building the app flow turned it into the main source of contracts, now over 80%.
