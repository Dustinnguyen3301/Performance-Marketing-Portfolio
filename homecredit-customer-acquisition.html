01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Cash Loan is Home Credit Vietnam’s flagship product: an unsecured consumer loan from 3 to 250 million VND, with approval in 3 minutes and disbursement within 10. My job was to bring in new Cash Loan customers online across many channels. I managed a budget of nearly 5 billion VND per month.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Role & Scope

Campaign Planning
Set acquisition goals with stakeholders and built the media plan, from channel mix to budget split.
Campaign Execution
Owned the acquisition KPIs and directed the agency team on launches and optimization.
Performance Monitoring & Analysis
Reviewed every channel daily and followed platform updates, so sudden changes and heavy competition hit results less.
Reporting & Creative Insights
Reported results to stakeholders and used the data to brief the agency on creative that brings in more contracts.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Master Channel Strategy
I split the user flow into two streams, web and app. Google was the main channel at 35% of the total budget, across several campaign types. TikTok came next at 25%, reaching potential borrowers through short video, then Facebook at 10%. The rest went to SEO, Zalo ZNS and affiliate vendors.
Audience Segmentation Strategy
I tested many audience types, mostly retargeting and lookalike, and helped segment Home Credit’s customer base by demographics, credit history and loan value, often stacking several conditions. With the tech team, we set up three ways to build and refresh audiences so less of it was done by hand: manual upload, in-app events and API from Big Data.
Search Behavior & Customer Journey Optimization
I had the agency reach people both while browsing and while searching, from native ads to search ads. On search, I managed four keyword groups: brand, product, category and competitor.
Creative Variety Testing
With stakeholders, I built creative around product USPs, promotions and schemes, and benefit updates. We tested the angles and put more budget behind the winners.
04 / EVIDENCE

The Data Behind the Scale-Up

Back-end attributed data, May 2024 to October 2026. In August 2025 we added an app flow that runs alongside the existing web flow, and optimized it on in-app events. From that month, approved loans (our qualified leads) and contracts both stepped up and stayed there, while the cost per contract went down.

05 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

+41% Cash Loan contracts: 65K through September 2026 vs. 46K in all of 2025
Total Cash Loan contracts reached 65K in the first nine months of 2026, already above the 46K of the whole of 2025. Marketing also set a record month of 8,160 contracts, against a previous average of about 3,900.
Over 10% of total marketing budget saved
Tuning creative and audiences in the app flow got us to KPI earlier than planned, which saved over 10% of the total marketing budget.
New app flow now delivers over 80% of contracts
Until June 2025 the program ran almost entirely on the web flow, through Google SEM and Performance Max. I added an app flow that runs alongside the web flow, and it now brings in over 80% of contracts.

Key learning: the biggest gain came from opening a second place to convert, not from tuning bids. The app flow I added alongside the web flow now accounts for over 80% of contracts.
