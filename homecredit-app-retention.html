PROJECT / APP RETENTION

01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

“Home App” is Home Credit Vietnam’s customer app. People use it to check product news, manage their contracts, sign up as new customers, and pay loan installments plus electricity, water and mobile data bills. My goal was to keep monthly active users (MAU) high and get them doing more in the app.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Role & Scope

Campaign Planning
Set app retention targets with stakeholders, then chose the channels and split the budget.
Campaign Execution
Owned the app KPIs and led the agency team through launches and ongoing optimization.
Performance Monitoring & Analysis
Checked every channel daily and kept up with ad platform changes, so sudden shifts and competitor moves hit results less.
Reporting & Creative Insights
Presented performance reports to stakeholders, and worked with the agency on creative that gets users to do more in the app.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Master Channel Strategy
I split the program into two streams: bringing back customers who already had the app, and getting installs or re-installs. About 40% of the budget went to Google (the widest app reach), 35% to TikTok, and the rest to Facebook and the app vendor.
Audience Segmentation Strategy
I tested and ran ads on audiences built from in-app behavior and engagement level: users who logged in within the last 30, 60 or 90 days, users who made a repayment in the app in the last 90 days, users who signed a contract but never repaid in the app, users with an active contract, and more. I used SAS RTDM to build these segments in real time, refresh them automatically, and sync them by API from Big Data to Google Ads and TikTok Ads.
Campaign Optimization
I had the agency test several campaign types and objectives, and read the results with both real-time and lifetime-value data. That raised the login rate and the number of actions after login, which grew monthly active users.
Creative Variety Testing
With stakeholders, I built creative around product USPs, promotions and schemes, and benefit updates. We tested the angles and put more budget behind the winners.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

+25% monthly active users
In Q1 2025, total monthly active users grew 25% versus the same period last year, with the peak month reaching 1.2M users.
30% lower cost per login on Google
Testing many audience types on Google, the main channel, cut cost per login by 30%.
Scaled TikTok budget 3x, with cost per login up only 15%
TikTok was a new channel for the app. I tripled its app budget while cost per login rose only 15%.

Key learning: re-engaging existing users is mostly an audience problem. Once segments reflected real in-app behavior, Google got cheaper and TikTok could scale 3x with cost per login up only 15%.
