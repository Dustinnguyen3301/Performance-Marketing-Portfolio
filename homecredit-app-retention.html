PROJECT / APP RETENTION

Home Credit

GOOGLE · TIKTOK · FACEBOOK · APP VENDOR
JUN 2024 → MAY 2025

+25%Monthly Active UsersQ1 2025 vs. Q1 2024
01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Home Credit Vietnam runs “Home App”, its official app for supporting and caring for customers. In the app, customers can follow the latest information on each product, manage all their contracts, apply to become a Home Credit customer, and pay their loans, electricity, water and mobile data from their phones. The goal was to keep monthly active users (MAU) high and encourage them to take more actions in the app.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Role & Scope

Campaign Planning
Worked directly with stakeholders to set the app retention objectives and shape the media strategy, including channel selection and budget allocation.
Campaign Execution
Took ownership of the app KPIs and led the agency team to launch campaigns and keep improving results through ongoing optimization.
Performance Monitoring & Analysis
Checked performance across every channel each day, stayed current with ad-platform changes, and reduced the impact of sudden shifts and competitive pressure.
Reporting & Creative Insights
Presented full performance reports to stakeholders, and worked with the agency team to surface creative insights that increase in-app user actions.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Master Channel Strategy
I split the program into two streams: re-engaging customers who already have the app, and triggering installs or re-installs. Budget went about 40% to Google (the broadest app ecosystem), 35% to TikTok, and the remainder to Facebook and the app vendor.
Audience Segmentation Strategy
I tested and ran ads on audiences built from in-app behavior and engagement level: users who logged in within the last 30, 60 or 90 days, users who made a repayment in the app in the last 90 days, users who signed a contract but never repaid in the app, users with an active contract, and more. I leveraged SAS RTDM to automate real-time audience segmentation, dynamic refreshing, and API synchronization from Big Data to Google Ads and TikTok Ads.
Campaign Optimization
I led the agency team in testing multiple campaign types and objectives, combining real-time and lifetime-value data in the analysis. This improved the app login rate and the actions users take after logging in, and increased monthly active users.
Creative Variety Testing
I gathered insights and worked with stakeholders to develop varied creative angles: product USPs, promotions and schemes, and benefit updates. Testing identified the winning content, which we then scaled efficiently.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

+25% monthly active users
In Q1 2025, total monthly active users grew 25% versus the same period last year, with the peak month reaching 1.2M users.
30% lower cost per login on Google
Applying a wide range of audience types improved cost per login by 30% on Google, the key channel.
Scaled TikTok budget 3x, with cost per login up only 15%
I explored TikTok as a new channel and found a way to scale its app budget 3x while cost per login rose by only 15%.

Key learning: behavior-based audience segmentation lowered cost per login on Google, the key channel, and the same testing discipline let us scale TikTok 3x with only a 15% rise in cost per login.
