HOW I WORK

From audit
to business results.

The same five steps on every account, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Audit

    Review the account, tracking and past data to find the real business problem before spending more.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Set objectives and KPIs, split the budget across channels and define audiences and creative tests.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Build the campaign structure, QA tracking end to end, and go live with clear test hypotheses.

  4. 04

    Optimize

    Weekly decisions on bids, budgets, audiences and creatives, driven by what the data shows.

  5. 05

    Report

    Clear reports that tie spend to business results, with the next actions already decided.
