Home Credit

01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Home Credit positions itself as a “dedicated digital finance” company that stays with customers throughout their journey. The key goals were to build visibility, make Home Credit “the top-of-mind choice” for fast, safe and transparent consumer finance, and grow its social media channels into important brand assets. These channels were used to share useful messages, care for customers and collect feedback to improve service.

I took responsibility for increasing reach and engagemen ability and grow follower, improve SOV of Home Credit.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Roles & Scopes

Campaign Planning
Work directly with stakeholders (Brand Team) to define clarity brand positioning, marketing objectives and develop the media specific strategy, including channel selection and budget allocation.
Campaign Execution
Take ownership for full KPI and manage agency to launching adsvertising campaigns, and apply tactics for optimizing performance
Performance Monitoring & Analyzing
Daily tracking total performance on all channels, update new information from ads platform and limit impact from big changes, high market competetion.
Reporting & Creative Insight Updating
Be directly deliver full marketing report to stakeholders, for updating performance status. Working with agency team to find new creative's insights and feedback for improving the user's engagment ability.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Channel & Budget Strategy
Over 50% of total budget for growing the reach & user's engagement on the Facebook (the social media was most used by Home Credit customer), alongside 25% of budget for increaing view & follower for TikTok Channel. The remain budget will be used for variety visibility to customer across platforms.
Audience & Traffic Strategy
Apply & customize ads for many audience's types, generally from mass to retargeting to look-a-like audience. Including the retargeting audience, i had deeply segment by user's behavior such as product view, add-to-cart, check-out, purchase to optimize the efficiency.
Campaign Optimization
For E-commerce campaigns, i priorities approach to customer through brand keyword to maximize visibility on touchpoint of customer journey, beside optimize impression share of product keyword related to Logitech products. In the livestream campaigns, i had test a lot of goals combine to gain more livestream watch and view product, which help improve the purchase rate.
Creative & Conversion Strategy
Provide key insights & get aligned with the Logitech manager to developed a range of voucher offers to enhance customer value and simplify the purchase experience
Examples: Voucher discount 30% (maximize: VND 150K) for ordered had value > 1M.
