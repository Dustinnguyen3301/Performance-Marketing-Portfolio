PROJECT / BRAND AWARENESS

Home Credit

01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Home Credit positions itself as a “dedicated digital finance” company that stays with customers throughout their journey. The key goals were to build visibility, make Home Credit “the top-of-mind choice” for fast, safe and transparent consumer finance, and grow its social media channels into important brand assets. These channels were used to share useful messages, care for customers and collect feedback to improve service.

I was responsible for increasing reach, engagement and followers, and for improving Home Credit’s brand search visibility, measured by search impression share.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Role & Scope

Campaign Planning
Worked directly with the Brand Team to clarify brand positioning and marketing objectives, and developed the media strategy, including channel selection and budget allocation.
Campaign Execution
Owned the full set of KPIs and managed the agency to launch advertising campaigns and apply optimization tactics.
Performance Monitoring & Analysis
Tracked performance across all channels daily, kept up with ad-platform updates, and limited the impact of sudden changes and intense market competition.
Reporting & Creative Insights
Delivered full marketing reports directly to stakeholders to update performance status, and worked with the agency team to find new creative insights and feedback that improve user engagement.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Channel & Budget Strategy
Home Credit owns two social media channels (Facebook and TikTok), each with over 1 million followers. I allocated about 65% of the total budget to these two channels to maximize brand visibility and deliver useful information quickly, and the remaining 35% to other channels to extend impressions.
Audience Overlap & Frequency Control
Because one person often uses several social platforms, I mapped key touchpoints along the user journey and each audience’s social media habits, then applied segmentation and exclusion tactics to limit audience overlap between channels. This kept the message present at every stage of the journey at the right frequency, avoiding over-exposure and ad fatigue, and let campaigns reach a larger pool of users while increasing engagement and positive interaction.
Campaign Optimization
I led the agency team in testing multiple campaign types and new ad-platform products, such as balancing auction and reservation campaigns, to reach more users while controlling frequency and improving CPM.
Creative Variety by Demographic
I gave the Brand Team insights to develop varied creative angles: product news, ESG, vouchers, community events, seasonal moments and holidays. Branding content about the work environment and community activities strengthened Home Credit’s image as a responsible brand growing with the community, raised brand recognition, and supported its competitiveness in top-workplace rankings.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

+125% total user reach across platforms
Facebook reach grew 130% and TikTok reach grew 115% versus 2024, while total CPM fell 5%.
+77% new TikTok followers, with over 1 million on each page
New followers of the Home Credit Vietnam TikTok page (ads and organic) rose from 35K in 2024 to 62K in 2025, while cost per follower improved 8%. Both the TikTok and Facebook pages now have over 1 million followers each.
+21% engagement rate on Facebook
Engagement rate rose from 3.75% in 2024 to 4.53% in 2025, driven by controlled budget allocation and creative tailored to each demographic segment.
Over 20% higher deeper view rate on TikTok and YouTube
Through analysis and audience insights, I worked closely with the agency to improve creative quality (format, hook, voice, etc.), lifting the deeper view rate on both platforms by over 20% versus 2024.
Brand search impression share up from 78% to 90%
When users searched for Home Credit brand keywords, impression share rose from 78% in 2024 to 90% in 2025, so Home Credit appeared in more of the searches where people looked for the brand.

Key learning: controlling audience overlap and tailoring creative by segment grew reach and engagement at the same time, without raising CPM.
