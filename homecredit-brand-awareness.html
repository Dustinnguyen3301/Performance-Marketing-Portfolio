PROJECT / BRAND AWARENESS

Home Credit

FACEBOOK · YOUTUBE · TIKTOK · ZALO · THREADS
FEB 2025 → 2026

+160%Brand Awareness Uplift2025 vs. 2024
01 / CONTEXT

Background & Objectives

Home Credit positions itself as a “dedicated digital finance” company that stays with customers throughout their journey. The key goals were to build visibility, make Home Credit “the top-of-mind choice” for fast, safe and transparent consumer finance, and grow its social media channels into important brand assets. These channels were used to share useful messages, care for customers and collect feedback to improve service.

I took responsibility for increasing reach and engagement ability and grow follower, improve SOV of Home Credit.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My Roles & Scopes

Campaign Planning
Work directly with stakeholders (Brand Team) to define clarity brand positioning, marketing objectives and develop the media specific strategy, including channel selection and budget allocation.
Campaign Execution
Take ownership for full KPI and manage agency to launching advertising campaigns, and apply tactics for optimizing performance
Performance Monitoring & Analyzing
Daily tracking total performance on all channels, update new information from ads platform and limit impact from big changes, high market competition.
Reporting & Creative Insight Updating
Be directly deliver full marketing report to stakeholders, for updating performance status. Working with agency team to find new creative's insights and feedback for improving the user's engagement ability.
03 / APPROACH

Strategy & Execution

Channel & Budget Strategy
Home Credit owned 2 social media channels (Facebook & TikTok) with over 1 million followers, I decided allocate nearly 65% of total budget for that channels with maximizing brand visibility to customers and using them as to deliver useful information fast & immediately, alongside 35% of budget for other channels to varify impression.
Overlapping Control Strategy
I applied a lot of targeting tactics related to segmentation and customize user journey to define key touchpoint and their using social media behavior. Then I & team action exclusion tactic to ensure information didn't appear too much and saturation. That help marketing campaign can deliver content to reach larger user's pool and increase the engagement rate & positive interaction.
Campaign Optimization
I lead the agency team take action and testing a lot campaign's type, new product from ads platform such as balance between auction and reservation campaign to achieve reach more users in pool with control frequency and improve the CPM.
Variety Creative By Demographic
Provide key insights to Brand Team to operate variety creative angle from product news, ESG, Voucher, Community event, seasonal, Holiday, etc. That help the image of Home Credit in user's mind with responsibility spirit of Home in community growing and increase the user's experience, raise ability to reward competition.
04 / IMPACT

Results & Learnings

+125% Total user reach across platforms
The user reach on Facebook in 2025 uplift by 130% and on TikTok uplift 115% versus 2024. While the total CPM reduced 5%.
+165% user follow with over 1 million follower on 2 social pages.
In 2025, the TikTok users follow TikTok Home Credit Vietnam increase 170% with cost per follower improved 8%. That help increase the amount of follower on TikTok Page and Facebook Page over 1 million.
Improved 20% the engagement rate and increase positive interaction on Facebook.
Applied tactic control budget allocation and customize creative for each demographic segments that help improve the Engagement Rate from 3.75% in 2024 to 4.53% in 2025.
Improved over 20% the View Rate on both TikTok and YouTube
With a lot of analysis and get insight, I work closely to improve the creative's quality (format, hook, voice, etc.) that help improved the deeper view rate on TikTok and YouTube had uplift by 20% (vs 2024).
